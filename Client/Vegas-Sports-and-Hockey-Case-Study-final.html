The client

A family business with 28 years of Las Vegas behind it

Vegas Sports & Hockey is a licensed sports shop in Las Vegas, founded in 1998 and still family owned. Online it trades as Uniform Sports. It carries jerseys, fitted caps, tees, hoodies and collectibles across the NFL, NHL, MLB, NBA, NCAA and more, with a strong local focus on the Vegas Golden Knights, Las Vegas Raiders and Las Vegas Aviators.

Our scope covered the whole journey: a Shopify rebrand and theme upgrade, a CRO audit with a first round of changes, then paid and organic growth on the new foundation.

The build

Six workstreams, each setting up the next

The foundation came first, conversion second and paid traffic third, so every step made the next one pay back more.

Shopify Rebranding

Brand and storefront redesign

A new visual identity and a storefront that speaks to fans in their own terms.

  • One brand voice across store, search listings and ads
  • A homepage organized around league, brand and category
  • Trust messaging for shipping, returns and authenticity
Shopify Development

Theme upgrade in Shopify

A custom Online Store 2.0 theme sized for a catalog of league, team and brand pages.

  • League-first header navigation for hundreds of teams
  • Loyalty and Google review widgets on the storefront
  • Mobile-first layout, since two thirds of visits are on phones
CRO Audit and Changes

Conversion rate optimization

A funnel audit, then changes aimed at the steps where shoppers dropped.

  • Funnel review from session to cart to checkout
  • Device and landing page analysis in Shopify
  • Changes to product pages, navigation and the checkout path
PPC

Google Ads

Campaigns launched gradually from mid August 2026 and scaled as data came in.

  • Phased budget ramp instead of a day-one spend spike
  • Team, league and product intent themes
  • Weekly review of traffic against Shopify orders
  • Budget moved toward what converted
SEO

Ecommerce SEO

A catalog structure that lets team and league pages rank for how fans actually search.

  • Product structured data (JSON-LD) on product pages
  • Team and league collections as ranking targets
  • Brand name aligned across search listings
AI Visibility

AEO and GEO

Content and markup shaped so AI assistants and search summaries can read and cite the store.

  • Clear, citable facts: founding year, shipping, returns, authenticity
  • Product and brand data structured for machine reading
  • Brand entity consistency across the web

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why rebrand a Shopify store before running Google Ads?

Ads buy traffic, and a store only turns that traffic into orders if shoppers trust it and find products quickly. Fixing identity, navigation and trust signals first means every ad click lands on a page built to convert.

Does Commerce Pundit handle Shopify development, CRO, PPC and SEO together?

Yes. One team covers Shopify development, rebranding, theme upgrades, CRO audits and changes, Google Ads, SEO and AI visibility, so each channel works from the same product and conversion data.
